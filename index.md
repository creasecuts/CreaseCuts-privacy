CreaseCuts Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

CreaseCuts respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how CreaseCuts handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

CreaseCuts does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to us through the app, unless otherwise explicitly stated in a future update.

2. Files and Models

CreaseCuts allows you to import and work with 3D models and associated files, including STL, OBJ and supported texture files.

Your imported files, papercraft layouts, project settings and edits are processed and stored locally on your device, unless you choose to export, share or otherwise transfer them using a feature of your device or another service.

CreaseCuts does not upload your project files to our servers.

3. Purchases

Any purchases made through CreaseCuts are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details or other payment information.

Purchases are subject to Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policy.

4. External Links

CreaseCuts may contain links to external websites or services, including community and social media platforms. When you access these links, you are subject to the privacy policies and practices of those third-party services.

CreaseCuts does not control how these external services collect or process information.

5. Data Security

Since CreaseCuts does not collect or maintain personal information on our servers, we do not operate a server-side database of your personal information.

You are responsible for maintaining backups of your project files and exported documents.

6. Children’s Privacy

CreaseCuts does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users through the app.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to CreaseCuts, its features or applicable requirements. Updated versions will be made available through an appropriate public location.
